Breaking the Silence

Healing starts when someone knows they are not alone.

Padare Breaking the Silence – Zambia is a recovery and wellness initiative built around compassion, dignity, professional support and hope.

We believe recovery is more than stopping a behaviour. It is about restoring relationships, rebuilding confidence, discovering purpose and creating a healthier future.

Who We Are

A safe place for healing and transformation.

We create environments where people can speak, be heard, recover and rediscover hope.

Our Story

Padare Breaking the Silence – Zambia exists to create a compassionate space where people affected by addiction, trauma, grief and life challenges can find practical support and renewed hope.

Our Mission

To provide holistic recovery, counselling, education and community-based support that restores dignity, purpose and wellbeing.

Our Vision

Communities where people are free to heal, equipped to thrive and empowered to build hopeful futures.

Our Approach

The 6-Pillar Recovery Model

Recovery becomes stronger when the whole person is supported.

01

Professional Counselling

Confidential and compassionate counselling tailored to individual needs.

02

Recovery Coaching

Practical guidance that helps people build sustainable recovery routines.

03

Trauma Healing

Safe, supportive pathways for processing trauma, grief and difficult experiences.

04

Nature Therapy

Healing through nature, mindfulness, movement and peaceful outdoor environments.

05

Therapeutic Farming

Meaningful work with soil, plants and animals that develops confidence and life skills.

06

Community Reintegration

Supporting people as they rebuild relationships, independence and community connections.
